# Gatekeeper — 2-3 Minute Pitch Video Script & Storyboard
**IUB Innovation to Impact (i2i) Award Program | Student Stream**

> *This script is structured to be recorded as a direct, energetic 2.5-minute pitch video. Use screen recording overlays during the prototype section.*

---

## Pitch Overview

* **Target Duration:** 2 Minutes 30 Seconds
* **Format:** Presenter on camera + live screen recordings of the Gatekeeper platform
* **Tone:** Clear, direct, evidence-driven, solution-focused

---

## Timed Script & Storyboard

### [0:00 – 0:20] Segment 1: Project & Team Intro
**Visual:** Presenter standing clearly on camera in front of an IUB campus background or computer workstation. Project title overlay: *Gatekeeper — Student Stream*.

> **Speaker:**
> *"Hello i2i Committee! My name is [Lead Name], representing our student development team from the School of Engineering, Technology & Sciences at Independent University, Bangladesh. We are applying under the **Student Stream** with **Gatekeeper**—an automated campus event access control and digital credential verification platform."*

---

### [0:20 – 0:50] Segment 2: Specific User & Problem Statement
**Visual:** Cut to footage/photos of long student queues at university gates, event registration desks, or auditorium entrances.

> **Speaker:**
> *"Every semester, the IUB Division of Student Affairs and student clubs like the IUB Photography Club organize major events attracting hundreds of attendees. But manual paper lists and slow ID checks create massive gate bottlenecks—taking up to 45 seconds per student. Crowded entryways create safety hazards, paper passes get duplicated by gate crashers, and organizers spend days manually typing lists after events to issue certificates. Campus event management needed a modern, automated gate access standard."*

---

### [0:50 – 1:25] Segment 3: What Already Exists (Live Prototype Demonstration)
**Visual:** Screen recording of Gatekeeper web app in action:
1. Mobile camera scanning a QR pass (green tick, sound beep).
2. Kiosk self-check-in screen.
3. Live dashboard showing real-time check-in stats.
4. One-click PDF certificate generator & public verification page.

> **Speaker:**
> *"We didn't just design an idea—we built a complete, production-ready web application. Gatekeeper turns any smartphone camera into a high-speed gate scanner. Attendees present their encrypted QR ticket, and entry is verified in under two seconds with live duplicate protection. For self-entry desks, our Kiosk mode lets students tap and enter independently. Once the event ends, Gatekeeper automatically generates personalized PDF certificates and hosts a public verifier where employers can validate certificate authenticity instantly."*

---

### [1:25 – 2:05] Segment 4: Real Evidence & Why It Matters
**Visual:** Full-screen graphics showing key pilot performance metrics: **500+ Scans**, **1.8s Check-in Speed**, **99.4% Reliability**, **0 Fake Entries**.

> **Speaker:**
> *"And we have real proof. During live campus pilots—including IUBPC events and workshops—Gatekeeper processed over 500 attendees smoothly. Entry verification time dropped from 45 seconds down to 1.8 seconds per attendee, completely eliminating gate queues. Database synchronization across multiple volunteer scanning phones prevented 100% of duplicate re-entry attempts, and event coordinators saved over 15 hours of post-event administrative work."*

---

### [2:05 – 3:00] Segment 5: Support Requested, Outcome & Honest Risks
**Visual:** Presenter back on camera with key bullet points appearing on screen.

> **Speaker:**
> *"All intellectual property belongs 100% to our student team. We have addressed key operational risks by building offline scanner caching for network drops and strict data privacy role controls.*
>
> *We are requesting support from the i2i Committee for three specific outcomes:*
> 1. *Official DSA sanction to make Gatekeeper the standard event entry system across all IUB campus events.*
> 2. *Cloud infrastructure and SMS gateway hosting support.*
> 3. *i2i incubation mentoring to commercialize Gatekeeper as a SaaS platform for external university and event clients across Bangladesh.*
>
> *Thank you for your time, and we look forward to bringing Innovation to Impact at IUB!"*

---

## Recording Tips for the Team

1. **Audio Quality:** Use a clip-on lapel mic or quiet indoor room to ensure crystal clear voice delivery.
2. **Demonstration Visuals:** Record screen capture of the live Gatekeeper application in high resolution (1080p).
3. **Pacing:** Keep energy high and stick strictly to the 2:30 time window.
