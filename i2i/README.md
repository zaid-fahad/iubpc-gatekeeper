## How to Submit on `i2i.iub.edu.bd`

### Step 1: Online Form Fields (Copy & Paste from [`APPLICATION_DRAFT.md`](file:///Users/gm-ict/Documents/iubpc-gatekeeper/i2i/APPLICATION_DRAFT.md))
1. Open [i2i.iub.edu.bd](http://i2i.iub.edu.bd).
2. Select **Student Stream**.
3. Fill in Project Title: `Gatekeeper — Automated Campus Event Access Control and Digital Credential Verification Platform`.
4. Select School: `School of Engineering, Technology & Sciences (SETS)`.
5. Enter Lead and Team Member details.
6. Copy-paste responses for Sections 1–6 into the online form:
   - **Section 1: Problem / Need** *(Max 300 words)*
   - **Section 2: Final-Stage Output** *(Max 300 words)*
   - **Section 3: Readiness Evidence** *(Max 500 words)*
   - **Section 4: Impact Route** *(Max 500 words)*
   - **Section 5: Ownership & Risks** *(Max 300 words)*
   - **Section 6: Support Requested** *(Max 300 words)*

### Step 2: Upload Attachments
1. **Video Link (Optional):** Upload pitch video to YouTube as *Unlisted* and paste URL.
2. **One-Page Diagram:** Upload PDF or PNG render of `ONE_PAGE_DIAGRAM.md`.
3. **Evidence Files:** Upload `EVIDENCE_SUMMARY.md` or PDF exports of pilot logs and survey results (Max 10 MB per file, up to 8 files total).
